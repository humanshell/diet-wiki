An anti-inflammatory environment in the vasculature helps prevent the progression of atherosclerosis and the stabilization of plaques. Dietary patterns like the [[mediterranean-diet]] promote this environment through high intakes of antioxidants and [[omega-3-fatty-acids]].

### Related Concepts
Reducing [[systemic-inflammation]] is a primary mechanism through which anti-inflammatory environments are achieved.

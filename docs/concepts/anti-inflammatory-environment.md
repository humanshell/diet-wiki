An anti-inflammatory environment in the vasculature helps prevent the progression of atherosclerosis and the stabilization of plaques. Dietary patterns like the [mediterranean-diet](../entities/mediterranean-diet.md) promote this environment through high intakes of antioxidants and [omega-3-fatty-acids](../entities/omega-3-fatty-acids.md).

### Related Concepts
Reducing [systemic-inflammation](systemic-inflammation.md) is a primary mechanism through which anti-inflammatory environments are achieved.

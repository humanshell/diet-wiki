Dyslipidemia management involves the use of lifestyle modifications and [[lipid-lowering-therapy]] (like [[statin-therapy]]) to control abnormal lipid levels and reduce cardiovascular risk. The [[2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines]] provides the current standard for this management.

### Assessment Tools
Effective [[dyslipidemia-management]] requires accurate lipid quantification, covered under [[ldl-c-calculation-formulas]], and evaluation of [[residual-risk-assessment]] after primary targets are met.

Dyslipidemia management involves the use of lifestyle modifications and [lipid-lowering-therapy](lipid-lowering-therapy.md) (like [statin-therapy](../entities/statin-therapy.md)) to control abnormal lipid levels and reduce cardiovascular risk. The [2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines](../entities/2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines.md) provides the current standard for this management.

### Assessment Tools
Effective [dyslipidemia-management](dyslipidemia-management.md) requires accurate lipid quantification, covered under [ldl-c-calculation-formulas](../comparisons/ldl-c-calculation-formulas.md), and evaluation of [residual-risk-assessment](residual-risk-assessment.md) after primary targets are met.

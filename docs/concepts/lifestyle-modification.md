A broad term for changes in behavior and habits — diet, exercise, stress management — aimed at improving health outcomes and reducing cardiovascular risk. Lifestyle modification is a cornerstone of [secondary-cardiovascular-prevention](secondary-cardiovascular-prevention.md) alongside pharmacological therapy.

## Diet

The [mediterranean-diet](../entities/mediterranean-diet.md) is the most extensively studied dietary pattern for cardiovascular protection. Key dietary modifications include:

- Replacing saturated fats with unsaturated fats from [extra-virgin-olive-oil](../entities/extra-virgin-olive-oil.md), [tree-nuts](../entities/tree-nuts.md), and [fish](../entities/fish.md)
- Increasing [fiber](../entities/whole-grains.md), [potassium](../entities/potassium.md), and [magnesium](../entities/magnesium.md) through vegetables, legumes, and whole grains
- Reducing [sodium](../entities/sodium.md) intake and replacing salt with herbs and spices
- Limiting [red-meat](../entities/red-meat.md), refined carbohydrates, and added sugars

## Exercise

Regular physical activity — including both aerobic and resistance training — is independently associated with reduced cardiovascular mortality and improved quality of life after myocardial infarction.

**[Calisthenics](../entities/calisthenics.md)**, a form of bodyweight resistance training, is particularly well-suited for post-MI patients because it:

- Requires no equipment, eliminating access barriers
- Progresses naturally from low-intensity to advanced, matching the cardiac rehab trajectory
- Simultaneously develops muscular strength, cardiovascular endurance, and functional mobility
- Improves blood pressure, lipid profile, insulin sensitivity, and VO₂max
- Can be performed as circuit training for combined aerobic + resistance benefit

See the [calisthenics](../entities/calisthenics.md) entity page for full exercise guidelines, progressions, and safety principles.

## Stress Management

Chronic stress contributes to hypertension, systemic inflammation, and adverse cardiovascular outcomes. Stress reduction techniques that complement dietary and exercise modifications include:

- Mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR)
- Slow, diaphragmatic breathing
- Progressive muscle relaxation
- Adequate sleep hygiene (7–9 hours per night)

## Comprehensive Lifestyle Programs

The [ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial](../entities/ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial.md) demonstrated that intensive lifestyle modification — combining a very-low-fat vegetarian diet, moderate exercise, stress management, and social support — can promote [plaque-regression](plaque-regression.md) and reduce cardiac events.

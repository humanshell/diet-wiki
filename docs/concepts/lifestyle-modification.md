A broad term for changes in behavior and habits (diet, exercise, stress management) aimed at improving health outcomes. The [[ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial]] demonstrated that structured lifestyle modifications can be as effective as pharmacological interventions for certain outcomes like [[plaque-regression]].

### Clinical Evidence
The [[lifestyle-intervention-vs-usual-care]] comparison summarizes trial evidence for structured lifestyle programs versus standard care. [[secondary-cardiovascular-prevention]] frequently incorporates these modifications as a core pillar.

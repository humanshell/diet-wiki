A broad term for changes in behavior and habits (diet, exercise, stress management) aimed at improving health outcomes. The [ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial](../entities/ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial.md) demonstrated that structured lifestyle modifications can be as effective as pharmacological interventions for certain outcomes like [plaque-regression](plaque-regression.md).

### Clinical Evidence
The [lifestyle-intervention-vs-usual-care](../comparisons/lifestyle-intervention-vs-usual-care.md) comparison summarizes trial evidence for structured lifestyle programs versus standard care. [secondary-cardiovascular-prevention](secondary-cardiovascular-prevention.md) frequently incorporates these modifications as a core pillar.

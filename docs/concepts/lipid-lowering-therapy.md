Lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) encompasses pharmacological and lifestyle interventions aimed at reducing atherogenic lipoproteins in the blood. The cornerstone of modern LLT is [[statin-therapy]], though the [[2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines]] also emphasize [[dyslipidemia-management]] through dietary patterns and risk-stratified targets.

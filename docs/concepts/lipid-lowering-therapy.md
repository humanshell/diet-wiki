Lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) encompasses pharmacological and lifestyle interventions aimed at reducing atherogenic lipoproteins in the blood. The cornerstone of modern LLT is [statin-therapy](../entities/statin-therapy.md), though the [2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines](../entities/2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines.md) also emphasize [dyslipidemia-management](dyslipidemia-management.md) through dietary patterns and risk-stratified targets.

MACE is a composite endpoint used in clinical trials to measure the combined incidence of major cardiovascular events, such as myocardial infarction, ischemic stroke, and cardiovascular death. The [cordioprev-trial](../entities/cordioprev-trial.md) used this as a primary measure of efficacy.

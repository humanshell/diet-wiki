Plaque regression is the reversal or reduction of atherosclerotic plaque buildup in the arteries. The [ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial](../entities/ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial.md) provided clinical evidence that intensive [lifestyle-modification](lifestyle-modification.md) can achieve this, even in patients with severe coronary disease.

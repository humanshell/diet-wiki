Residual risk assessment involves identifying remaining cardiovascular risk in patients after they have achieved their primary lipid targets. Markers like [apolipoprotein-b](../entities/apolipoprotein-b.md) and [lipoprotein-a](../entities/lipoprotein-a.md) are used to assess residual atherogenic and inflammatory risk.

Risk stratification is the process of categorizing patients into different risk levels (e.g., low, intermediate, high, very high) to guide the intensity of [dyslipidemia-management](dyslipidemia-management.md). The [2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines](../entities/2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines.md) provides frameworks for this stratification.

The [2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines](../entities/2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines.md) provide the primary clinical framework for cardiovascular risk stratification based on LDL-C thresholds and overall ASCVD risk.

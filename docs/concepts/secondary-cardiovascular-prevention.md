Secondary cardiovascular prevention refers to the medical and lifestyle interventions implemented after a patient has experienced an initial cardiovascular event (such as a myocardial infarction or stroke) to prevent recurrence. Key components include aggressive lipid management, blood pressure control, and nutritional strategies like the [mediterranean-diet](../entities/mediterranean-diet.md).

Beyond pharmacological management, intensive [lifestyle-modification](lifestyle-modification.md) (as seen in the [ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial](../entities/ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial.md)) has also been shown to facilitate [plaque-regression](plaque-regression.md).

The [cordioprev-trial](../entities/cordioprev-trial.md) provided additional evidence that a Mediterranean-style diet can reduce the risk of recurrent MACE compared to low-fat alternatives.

Modern guidelines, such as the [2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines](../entities/2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines.md), emphasize aggressive [dyslipidemia-management](dyslipidemia-management.md) and [risk-stratification](risk-stratification.md) to reduce [atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease](../entities/atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease.md).

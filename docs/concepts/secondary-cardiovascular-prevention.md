Secondary cardiovascular prevention refers to the medical and lifestyle interventions implemented after a patient has experienced an initial cardiovascular event (such as a myocardial infarction or stroke) to prevent recurrence. Key components include aggressive lipid management, blood pressure control, and nutritional strategies like the [[mediterranean-diet]].

Beyond pharmacological management, intensive [[lifestyle-modification]] (as seen in the [[ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial]]) has also been shown to facilitate [[plaque-regression]].

The [[cordioprev-trial]] provided additional evidence that a Mediterranean-style diet can reduce the risk of recurrent MACE compared to low-fat alternatives.

Modern guidelines, such as the [[2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines]], emphasize aggressive [[dyslipidemia-management]] and [[risk-stratification]] to reduce [[atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease]].

Systemic inflammation is a key driver in the progression of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD). Biomarkers such as high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hsCRP) are used to monitor this state. Dietary patterns that reduce inflammation, such as the [[mediterranean-diet]], are critical in managing long-term risk and promoting [[secondary-cardiovascular-prevention]].

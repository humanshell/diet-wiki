The 2026 American College of Cardiology (ACC) and American Heart Association (AHA) Dyslipidemia Guidelines provide the current clinical standard for managing abnormal lipid levels and reducing cardiovascular risk. They recommend strict LDL-C treatment goals based on [risk-stratification](../concepts/risk-stratification.md), universal screening for [lipoprotein-a](lipoprotein-a.md), and the use of [apolipoprotein-b](apolipoprotein-b.md) for residual atherogenic risk assessment. The guidelines explicitly recommend against using unregulated dietary supplements like fish oils and red yeast rice.

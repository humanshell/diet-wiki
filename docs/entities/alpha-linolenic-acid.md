Alpha-Linolenic Acid (ALA) is an essential omega-3 fatty acid. The [lyon-diet-heart-study](lyon-diet-heart-study.md) highlighted its role in cardioprotection, particularly when consumed as part of a [mediterranean-diet](mediterranean-diet.md). ALA is found in high concentrations in foods like flaxseeds, chia seeds, hemp hearts, and canola oil.

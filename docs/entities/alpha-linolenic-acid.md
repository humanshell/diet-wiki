Alpha-Linolenic Acid (ALA) is an essential omega-3 fatty acid. The [[lyon-diet-heart-study]] highlighted its role in cardioprotection, particularly when consumed as part of a [[mediterranean-diet]]. ALA is found in high concentrations in foods like flaxseeds and canola oil.

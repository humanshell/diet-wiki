Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD) is a spectrum of conditions caused by the buildup of plaque in the arteries. It is the target of both [dyslipidemia-management](../concepts/dyslipidemia-management.md) and intensive [secondary-cardiovascular-prevention](../concepts/secondary-cardiovascular-prevention.md).

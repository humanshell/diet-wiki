The CORDIOPREV trial compared the efficacy of a Mediterranean diet against a low-fat diet in patients with coronary heart disease (CHD). The study found that the Mediterranean diet group had a 28.1% lower risk of recurrent [[major-adverse-cardiovascular-events]] (MACE).

### Additional Findings
- Kidney protection: The trial showed attenuated eGFR decline, relevant to [[chronic-kidney-disease]].

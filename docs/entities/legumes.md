Legumes—including beans, lentils, chickpeas, and peas—are plant-based protein sources foundational to the Mediterranean diet. They are rich in fiber, protein, and minerals while being low in saturated fat. The Mediterranean diet includes legumes daily or several times per week, providing plant-based protein alongside extra-virgin olive oil and vegetables.

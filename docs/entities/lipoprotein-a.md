Lipoprotein(a) [Lp(a)] is a genetically determined lipoprotein that increases cardiovascular risk. The [[2026-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guidelines]] recommends universal screening for Lp(a) at least once in adulthood.

These guidelines provide the clinical framework for managing Lp(a)-related risk alongside [[dyslipidemia-management]] strategies.

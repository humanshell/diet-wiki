The Lyon Diet Heart Study was a landmark randomized trial that demonstrated a 50% to 70% reduction in recurrent myocardial infarction and cardiac death in patients following a Mediterranean-style diet. The study emphasized the importance of [[alpha-linolenic-acid]] and demonstrated cardioprotection through non-lipid pathways.

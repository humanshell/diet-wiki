Nuts are nutrient-dense foods recommended in heart-healthy dietary patterns. The [[mediterranean-diet]] emphasizes nuts as a key component, and specific varieties like [[tree-nuts]] were featured in the [[cordioprev-trial]]. Nuts provide healthy unsaturated fats, protein, fiber, and micronutrients that support cardiovascular health. Replacing animal proteins with nuts and seeds may further reduce mortality risks.

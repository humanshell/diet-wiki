Nuts are nutrient-dense foods recommended in heart-healthy dietary patterns. The [mediterranean-diet](mediterranean-diet.md) emphasizes nuts as a key component, and specific varieties like [tree-nuts](tree-nuts.md) were featured in the [cordioprev-trial](cordioprev-trial.md). Nuts provide healthy unsaturated fats, protein, fiber, and micronutrients that support cardiovascular health. Replacing animal proteins with nuts and seeds may further reduce mortality risks.

The Ornish Lifestyle Heart Trial was a landmark randomized, controlled clinical trial that demonstrated the ability of intensive, non-pharmacological lifestyle modifications to induce regression of coronary atherosclerosis. Key components included a [very-low-fat-vegetarian-diet](very-low-fat-vegetarian-diet.md), aerobic exercise, stress management, and group support.

Red meat (beef, pork, lamb) is limited in the Mediterranean diet due to its high saturated fat content. The Mediterranean diet specifically limits red meat in favor of fish, poultry, and plant-based proteins. High consumption of red meat (>14g/day) and processed red meat has been associated with cardiovascular death.

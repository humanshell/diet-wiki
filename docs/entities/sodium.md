Sodium is a mineral that plays a key role in fluid balance. However, high sodium intake is strongly linked to increased blood pressure. In a Mediterranean-style dietary pattern, sodium reduction is achieved through cooking with herbs, garlic, citrus, and vinegar rather than added salt, and by avoiding processed and packaged foods.

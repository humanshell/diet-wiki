Statin therapy is a cornerstone of [dyslipidemia-management](../concepts/dyslipidemia-management.md) and [secondary-cardiovascular-prevention](../concepts/secondary-cardiovascular-prevention.md), primarily used to lower LDL-C and reduce the risk of [atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease](atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease.md).

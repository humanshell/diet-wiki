Vegetables are a cornerstone of the Mediterranean diet, which emphasizes vegetables as a foundational plant-based food. Vegetables are rich in [potassium](potassium.md), [magnesium](magnesium.md), fiber, and antioxidants with minimal caloric density, making them ideal for weight management and cardiovascular protection.

A dietary pattern characterized by extremely low fat intake and a focus on whole plant foods. This was the cornerstone of the [ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial](ornish-lifestyle-heart-trial.md) and was shown to contribute to [plaque-regression](../concepts/plaque-regression.md).

## The Mediterranean Pattern

The traditional Mediterranean dietary pattern is the most extensively studied eating style for cardiovascular protection. Rooted in the food traditions of Greece, Italy, Southern France, Spain, and the Levant during the 1950s–1960s, it emphasizes whole plant foods, healthy fats, and minimal processed foods.

**Core pillars:**

- **[[extra-virgin-olive-oil]]** — The primary fat source; rich in monounsaturated fatty acids and polyphenols that support endothelial function
- **[[vegetable|Vegetables]] and [[fruit|Fruits]]** — Daily staples, consumed in large quantities; provide potassium, fiber, and antioxidants
- **[[legumes]]** — Beans, lentils, and chickpeas; eaten daily or several times per week as a plant-based protein source
- **[[whole-grains]]** — Unprocessed cereals including wheat, barley, bulgur, and oats; the daily carbohydrate foundation
- **[[fish]]** — Especially fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel); the primary animal protein, rich in omega-3 fatty acids
- **Moderate [[cheese]] and [[wine]]** — Cheese and yogurt as regular condiments; wine in low-to-moderate amounts with meals
- **Limited [[red-meat]]** — Red meat is a few-times-per-month food, not a daily staple

**Key foods not yet covered:** [[mediterranean-herbs]] (parsley, basil, oregano, thyme, rosemary), [[poultry]], [[eggs]], [[cheese]], [[yogurt]], [[eggplant]], [[tahini]], [[canola-oil]]

## Core Concepts

Understanding the biological mechanisms that drive Mediterranean diet benefits:

- **[[systemic-inflammation]]** — Chronic low-grade inflammation drives atherosclerosis; the Med diet reduces inflammatory markers through polyphenols and omega-3 fatty acids
- **[[anti-inflammatory-environment]]** — The physiological state promoted by Med diet fats (ALA, polyphenols) and food choices; counteracts inflammatory pathways
- **[[anti-thrombotic-environment]]** — Med diet components (olive oil polyphenols, ALA, fish omega-3s) reduce platelet aggregation and thrombotic risk
- **[[blood-pressure-reduction]]** — Achieved through increased potassium, magnesium, and fiber from vegetables and legumes; sodium reduction through herb-based seasoning
- **[[dyslipidemia-management]]** — Mediterranean pattern improves the lipid profile: lowers triglycerides, raises HDL-C, and reduces atherogenic lipoprotein particles
- **[[plaque-regression]]** — Evidence from Ornish trial shows intensive lifestyle change can reduce non-calcified plaque volume

## Supporting Nutrients and Biomarkers

- **[[omega-3-fatty-acids]]** — EPA and DHA from fish; ALA from canola oil and walnuts
- **[[alpha-linolenic-acid]]** — Plant-based omega-3; the key cardioprotective nutrient in the Lyon study
- **[[lipoprotein-a]]** — Lp(a), an independent ASCVD risk marker elevated in many post-MI patients
- **[[apolipoprotein-b]]** — ApoB, the primary structural protein of atherogenic lipoprotein particles
- **[[sodium]]** — Key target for reduction; Mediterranean cooking replaces salt with herbs, garlic, and lemon
- **[[potassium]], [[magnesium]], [[calcium]]** — Electrolytes that support blood pressure regulation through vascular tone

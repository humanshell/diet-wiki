# Baba Ganoush (Authentic & Smoky)

A deliciously creamy and smoky roasted eggplant dip. The signature flavor comes from charring eggplants directly over an open flame — a traditional Middle Eastern technique.

- High in fiber (7g per serving) — supports digestive and cardiovascular health
- Charred skin contains antioxidants — don't peel it off

## Recipe Notes

- Gas stovetop charring is traditional and creates the signature smoky flavor
- If using broiler: char under high heat, turning frequently, for similar results
- **Do not skip the steaming step** — it removes bitter liquid and prevents stringy texture
- Serve with fresh-cut vegetables, whole grain pita, or as part of a mezze platter
- Stores well in the fridge for up to 4 days

## Serving Suggestions

- **Mezze platter**: Serve alongside hummus, olives, and fresh bread
- **Vegetable dip**: Fresh cut cucumbers, carrots, bell peppers
- **Sandwich spread**: Alternative to mayonnaise on Mediterranean sandwiches

ingredients:
  - "1 bunch parsley"
  - "3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil"
  - "1 medium yellow onion (finely chopped)"
  - "3 large garlic cloves (minced)"
  - "2 tablespoons tomato paste"
  - "2 large carrots (chopped)"
  - "2 celery stalks (chopped)"
  - "1 teaspoon Italian seasoning"
  - "1 teaspoon sweet paprika"
  - "½ teaspoon red pepper flakes (or Aleppo pepper, plus more for serving)"
  - "Kosher salt"
  - "Black pepper"
  - "4 cups (32 ounces) vegetable broth"
  - "2 cans Cannellini beans (drained and rinsed)"
  - "2 heaping cups spinach"
  - "¼ cup chopped fresh dill (stems removed)"
  - "2 tablespoons white wine vinegar"
instructions:
  - "Prep the parsley. Trim the bottom end of the parsley stems. Pick off the leaves and set aside in two separate piles — stems and leaves. Finely chop both."
  - "Sauté the aromatics. In a large Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium-high heat. Add onions and garlic. Cook, stirring regularly, for about 3 to 5 minutes or until fragrant."
  - "Add the remaining flavor-makers. Stir in tomato paste, carrots, celery, and chopped parsley stems (keep leaves aside). Season with Italian seasoning, paprika, Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes, and a big pinch of salt and pepper. Cook until vegetables soften, about 5 minutes."
  - "Add the vegetable broth and beans. Turn heat to high to bring to a boil and allow to boil for about 5 minutes."
  - "Simmer. Lower the heat and cover the pot part-way. Simmer for about 20 minutes, or until the beans and veggies are very soft."
  - "Partially blend for a creamier soup (optional). Use an immersion blender to blend about half the soup — do not fully puree. Some texture is essential."
  - "Finish. Stir in spinach and cover so it wilts (about 1 to 2 minutes). Stir in reserved parsley leaves, dill, and white wine vinegar."
  - "Serve. Ladle into serving bowls and finish each bowl with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of red pepper flakes or Aleppo pepper."

# Mediterranean White Bean Soup with Spinach and Fresh Herbs

A simple, weeknight-friendly soup loaded with bold Mediterranean flavor. Vegan and gluten-free, with a secret weapon: the fresh herb combination of dill and parsley creates an irresistible fresh-meets-cozy quality.

## Rating
★★★★½ **4.93/5** (103 ratings, 13 reviews) — [View on The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/white-bean-soup/)

## Why This Recipe Fits the Mediterranean Diet

- **[legumes](../entities/legumes.md)** (cannellini beans) — excellent plant-based protein, high in fiber (13.8g per serving)
- [vegetable](../entities/vegetable.md)-rich: spinach, carrots, celery, onion, garlic
- [extra-virgin-olive-oil](../entities/extra-virgin-olive-oil.md) as the cooking fat — Mediterranean diet foundation
- **Vegan and gluten-free** — suitable for most dietary needs
- Very low sodium (106mg per serving) — naturally heart-healthy
- White wine vinegar adds tang without added salt — supports blood pressure goals

## Recipe Notes

- **Don't skip the fresh herbs** — they are the signature element of this recipe
- Aleppo pepper is the preferred finishing spice — find it at Middle Eastern grocers or online
- Partial blending creates a creamier texture without adding cream or dairy
- Pairs excellently with crusty whole grain bread for a complete Mediterranean meal
- Freezes well for up to 3 months

## Variations

- **With chicken**: Add cooked shredded chicken for extra protein
- **Canned beans & BPA**: Look for organic BPA-free canned beans, or cook dried beans yourself

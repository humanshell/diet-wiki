# The Brunch Power Shake

Performance & Longevity Formula
This shake is a nutrient-dense, meal-replacement protocol designed to support joint health, cardiovascular function, and long-term vitality.

## **The Ingredients**

* **Protein Foundation:** 
1 scoop Whey Protein Concentrate + 1 scoop Multi-Collagen Complex (Bovine, Chicken, Fish, Egg).
 
* **The Liquid Base:** 
50/50 split of Skim Milk and Water (to balance texture and sugar content).
 
* **Healthy Fats:** 
1–2 tbsp High-Quality Organic Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO), 1 tbsp Chia Seeds, 1 tbsp Hemp Hearts.(1 tbsp of Psyllium Husk for even more fiber)
 
* **Flavor & Antioxidants:** 
Cocoa Powder, 1 Banana, Handful of Blueberries, Handful of Blackberries, 1–2 tbsp Peanut Butter Powder (adds protein too).

* **Performance/Digestive:** 
1 serving Creatine, 2–3 tbsp Yogurt.

## **Core Health Benefits**
 
* **Joint, Skin, Hair & Nail Support:** 
The high concentrations of **Glycine, Proline, and Hydroxyproline** from the collagen complex provide the necessary structural materials. The **Vitamin C** from the berries acts as the essential "bridge" that allows your body to synthesize this collagen into functional tissue.
 
* **Cardiovascular Health (BP & Cholesterol):** * 

**EVOO:** The polyphenols (specifically *oleocanthal*) help reduce systemic inflammation and improve blood vessel elasticity.
  
* **Omega-3s:** 
Chia and hemp hearts assist in regulating triglycerides.
 
* **Fiber:** 
Soluble fiber from the seeds, psyllium husk, and fruit supports healthy cholesterol excretion.
 
* **Steady Energy & Satiety:** 
By using a 50/50 milk-to-water ratio, you keep the shake refreshing while the fat/fiber complex ensures slow, steady digestion, preventing the insulin spikes common in standard meal replacements.
 
* **Recovery & Performance:** 
The whey and creatine combination ensures your muscles receive a steady supply of amino acids for repair, while the creatine aids in power output and ATP regeneration.
